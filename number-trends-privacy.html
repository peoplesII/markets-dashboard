Number Trends - Privacy Policy & App Information

Last updated: October 13, 2026
Application: Number Trends (sk.peoplesii.numbertrends)
Developer: Peoplesii
Privacy and support contact: sk.gape@gmail.com

Privacy at a glance: Number combinations and optional date-based personalization are calculated locally. The developer does not automatically receive your personal-date input or maintain a server database of these dates. Optional Premium is paid through Google Play. Sending a support message or sharing a file is your choice.

1. About the App and This Policy

Number Trends is an independent mathematical, statistical and probability-analysis tool for number combinations and historical datasets, including the EJ dataset. It can generate combinations and compare them with historical records, summarize frequencies and display statistical trends. The calculations run locally; they do not require sending your combinations to an external AI service.

This policy explains local app processing, optional purchases and user-initiated support or sharing. It applies to Number Trends with the application identifier above. Statistical outputs are informational; historical patterns do not guarantee future results.

2. Independent App and Software Payments

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or acting on behalf of an organizer of number games or an external historical-data provider. It does not sell game entries, accept wagers or deposits for game participation, transmit participation orders, manage game balances or pay prizes. A calculation amount entered in the app is a local analytical parameter, not a payment or deposit.

Optional Premium pays only for additional software features. Google Play handles that purchase; it is separate from participation in any external activity.

3. Data Stored and Used Locally

The app may store the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Language, display and calculation settings, analytical profiles and calculation seeds.
  • Generated AI or Hybrid number combinations, their history and comparison results.
  • Downloaded public historical number records and manually entered records.
  • Imported backup data and local calculation memory used to compare previous combinations.
  • An optional date and its selected type, when you enable date-based personalization.
  • Technical events, error information and diagnostic data described below.

The app has no developer-operated cloud synchronization of this data. The developer cannot remotely access your private app storage. Number Trends does not sell user data and does not include advertising networks or a developer-operated remote analytics service in the current release.

4. Optional Personal Date

If available in your plan and enabled by you, a date can be used locally to derive suggested numbers. Its type can be your birth date, a child's birth date, an anniversary or another important date. A name, identity document or contact record is not required.

This date is not transmitted to the developer or used for advertising. It is not included in the settings diagnostic summary or the generated-plan diagnostic summary. The raw date-based personalization report is excluded from the support attachment. These exclusions are implemented in the updated release prepared with this policy. Older test builds may use a different diagnostic format; use a newly built updated version before sending diagnostics.

Disabling the layer stops its use in calculations but does not automatically erase a previously saved date. You can erase private app data through Android settings. Provide only information you intend to keep locally and avoid unnecessary information about other people.

5. Technical Diagnostics and Voluntary Support

Technical events can be recorded locally while the app is used. A report is shared only when you choose a sharing action and a receiving app. Opening the share chooser does not automatically send an email.

A technical report may contain:

  • App and calculation-module versions, installation source and app-signature information.
  • Device manufacturer, model, supported architectures, Android version and security patch.
  • Language, time zone, storage availability, calculation settings and seeds.
  • Recent technical events, public historical-data status, errors and process-exit or memory information.
  • Descriptive analysis or audit information and a restricted plan summary: EJ, combination count and permitted profile.

The personal date entered in the optional date feature and purchase tokens are not included in this diagnostic summary. Public dataset dates and technical timestamps are different from a personal-date input and may appear in technical records.

If you send an email, the developer receives the sender address, the message and any attachment you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request, investigate a reported issue or provide purchase support, not for advertising. Do not add personal dates, passwords or payment-card details to a support message.

6. Premium Purchases Through Google Play

Premium is optional prepaid access for one year, without automatic renewal. The actual price and currency are shown by Google Play before confirmation. Free access remains available without a Premium purchase.

Google Play processes the payment and maintains its purchase records. The app uses Google Play Billing to receive product and purchase identifiers, purchase status and acknowledgement status, and to check whether Premium is active. Purchase tokens are used in communication with Google Play; they are not sent to a developer purchase-verification server or written to the app's diagnostic log. Verified access is held temporarily in app memory rather than as a permanent paid-access flag.

The app does not receive your payment-card details, banking credentials or Google password. Google Play may make limited order information available to the developer for purchase support, refunds or applicable accounting obligations. Google manages its own records under its Privacy Policy.

Checking or restoring Premium requires an internet connection and the Google Play account used for the purchase. You can restore it on a new phone with that account. Local combination history is transferred separately through export and import. Removing the app does not erase Google Play purchase records.

7. Internet Connections and This Website

The app connects to public data providers, including TIPOS, to download historical number records, and to Google Play for optional purchases and purchase verification. These services receive the network request and may process connection information such as an IP address and technical request metadata under their own policies. Your optional personal-date input is not included in the historical-data request.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. This page does not add analytics scripts, advertising tags or forms that collect app data. GitHub may process website-visit information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening third-party links or choosing another app to share a file is subject to that service's policies.

8. Exports, Imports and Sharing

Exports and backups are created at your request and can contain analytical history, combinations, results, public dataset records and calculation metadata. They are not automatically uploaded by the app to the developer. You choose the destination, which may be local storage, an email app or a cloud-storage provider. Imported files are read to restore local analytical data.

Keep exported files secure. Deleting private app storage does not delete files saved elsewhere, files already sent or copies held by a receiving service.

9. Retention and Deletion

Private app data remains on your device until it is removed by you or Android. Clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling removes the app's private local data. Remove exported files and copies in other apps or cloud storage separately. The app does not automatically back up its private data to a developer cloud account.

If you voluntarily contact support, correspondence and attachments are retained only as needed to handle the request and justified follow-up, or to meet an applicable legal obligation. You can request deletion of information you sent to the developer at sk.gape@gmail.com. Google Play and other receiving services maintain their records under their own policies.

Number Trends does not create a separate online app account, so there is no developer-operated app account to delete. Your Google account is managed by Google. The developer cannot remotely erase your device data or copies held by other services.

10. Security and Permissions

App network requests use HTTPS and unencrypted HTTP is blocked by the app configuration. Private app storage is protected by Android's application sandbox. Only files you choose to share are made available to the receiving app through the sharing flow. Exported files require your own protection at the chosen destination.

The app does not request access to contacts, SMS, call logs, precise device location, photos, camera or microphone for its analytical features. No software can guarantee complete protection on a compromised device. Keep Android updated and install signed builds from trusted sources.

11. Privacy Requests

Where applicable, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information you voluntarily sent to the developer, or exercise other rights under applicable data-protection law, including restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. You may also raise a concern with your competent data-protection authority. Use the privacy contact below; provide only the information needed to handle your request.

12. Google Play Data Safety

Local calculations do not automatically send your combinations or personal-date input to the developer. Google Play purchase processing, network requests and information you voluntarily share for support are separate from these local calculations. This policy describes those differences; it does not imply that Google Play or a receiving email or file-sharing service processes no information.

13. Audience and Policy Changes

Number Trends is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. An adult may choose a date such as a child's birth date for the optional local feature; this does not create a child account or send that date to the developer.

This policy may be updated when functionality, data handling or applicable requirements change. The latest published version will remain available at this address. Statements about Premium describe the annual prepaid product when it is available; product availability is determined by Google Play.
